## 5. Reddit r/FantasyPL — rules and safe approach

Reddit's direct page couldn't be fetched (access denied), so the following is based on general Reddit guidelines and FPL community knowledge.

### What Reddit's site-wide rules say (2026)

Reddit retired the formal "10% self-promotion" page but the principle remains: **be a genuine participant, not just a promoter.** Moderators judge accounts on overall behaviour. Posting the same link across multiple accounts or only self-promoting is a bannable offence. Account age and karma matter — new accounts that immediately post product links are flagged.

### What to expect on r/FantasyPL specifically

r/FantasyPL has its own rules in the sidebar (check directly before posting). Large FPL subreddits typically:

- Allow **self-promotion in dedicated weekly threads** (e.g., a "show your FPL tools" or "app dev" megathread — check if one exists)
- **Ban cold product links** as standalone posts
- May permit a launch post if it's framed as a genuine FPL player sharing something they built, with community value front-and-centre

### Safe community strategy

1. **Start now, not at launch.** The co-founder's 6,000 Twitter followers are the primary lever. On Reddit, build karma first by contributing genuinely — answer transfer questions, post GW analysis, share data insights — for 4-6 weeks before any product mention.
2. **Value-first post, app secondary.** The safest Reddit launch format is a data/analysis post where the insight is the value ("Here's how live rank moved in GW1 for players who captained X") with "I built SimplyFPL to track this" near the bottom.
3. **One launch post, then step back.** Don't cross-post to multiple FPL subreddits in the same window. One good post beats three flagged ones.
4. **Check the rules sidebar directly** at reddit.com/r/FantasyPL before posting — mod rules vary and change between seasons.
5. **Discord is lower-risk.** Individual FPL Discord servers are more permissive about tool sharing. The FPL Focal server (discord.me/fplfocal) and the servers listed at top.gg/discord/servers/tag/fpl are good starting points. DM a moderator first.

**Sources:** [redship.io](https://redship.io/blog/reddit-self-promotion-rules) · [karmaguy.io](https://karmaguy.io/en/blog/reddit-self-promotion-rules) · [reddit-radar-marketing.com](https://www.reddit-radar-marketing.com/blog/reddit-product-launch-strategy)

## 6. Recommendation

**Start with Typefully + Canva Pro.**

| Tool              | Cost       | Role                                                          |
| ----------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Typefully Starter | $8/mo      | Write, schedule, and AI-assist X/Twitter content              |
| Canva Pro         | $15/mo     | Match-day graphics, player stat cards, GW deadline countdowns |
| **Total**         | **$23/mo** |                                                               |

**Why this stack:**

- Both tools have very low learning curves — a non-technical co-founder can be productive in under an hour.
- Typefully's AI assistant learns the co-founder's voice over time; content creation gets faster each week, not slower.
- Canva has football match-day templates ready out of the box. Brand Kit locks in SimplyFPL colours once and every graphic from then on is consistent.
- The total cost is negligible relative to even a small number of Pro subscribers.
- Both support a batch workflow — 2-3 hours on a Sunday writing and scheduling the week's content, then hands-off.

**Add FeedHive later if:** content recycling becomes important mid-season (repeating GW patterns mean good content from GW5 can resurface in GW25 with a variation). At launch it adds complexity without enough benefit.

**Add Canva AI / Adobe Firefly later if:** the visual bar needs to rise. Firefly is the better generator for highly custom graphics but requires Photoshop familiarity. Canva Dream Lab is sufficient for launch.

**Skip Postwise, Hypefury, HeyGen, Opus Clip, AdCreative.ai** at launch. Each is either too expensive, too niche, or adds overhead the co-founder won't have once the full-time job starts on 1 August.

## 7. GW1 launch-week content plan

Assumes co-founder has set up Typefully + Canva, batch-writes on the Sunday before GW1, then the week runs largely on autopilot. Each day: ~10-15 minutes of live monitoring for breaking team news; otherwise scheduled.

### Sunday (6 days before GW1 deadline)

**Canva:** Create 3 reusable templates in your brand:

1. "Deadline day countdown" card (update the number each day)
2. "We're live — SimplyFPL GW1" launch graphic
3. "Live rank updating" animated-style card (static is fine)

**Typefully batch:**

- Thread: "SimplyFPL is live. Here's what's different from other FPL tools: [3-4 bullet points about speed, live rank, Frank AI]. Free at simplyfpl.com → [link]"
- Single tweet: "GW1 deadline is [date]. We'll be live-tracking rank from the first kick-off. [link]"
- Single tweet: "Frank (our AI) called [captain choice] for GW1. Here's why: [2-line summary]. Full picks → [link]"

Schedule these across Mon–Tue–Wed.

### Monday

**Post:** Launch announcement thread (scheduled Sunday). Reply to FPL accounts you follow — engage in GW1 captain debates naturally, don't force the app into every reply.

### Tuesday

**Post:** Deadline countdown card ("5 days to GW1 deadline") + "This is the moment live rank matters most. SimplyFPL updates every 60 seconds. [link]"

### Wednesday — GW1 Deadline day

**Post:** "Deadline day. Last chance to make changes. Frank's final GW1 picks: [captain / vice-captain]. See live rank from first kick-off → [link]"

Engage actively in the FPL Twitter deadline-day conversation. Reply to big FPL accounts' deadline posts with something useful (not a link dump — save links for own posts).

**Reddit:** Post the value-first post to r/FantasyPL if karma allows: "GW1 captain data — what the top 10k are doing this week" (pull real data from SimplyFPL / Frank), mention the tool naturally at the bottom.

### Thursday–Friday — Match days

**Live:** Monitor feed. Canva "live rank updating" card posted 30 mins after first kick-off: "Live rank is live. [link] — updating every 60 seconds."

**Batch (scheduled Wednesday):** "Bonus point alert — [player] has provisional bonus. Live rank moving → [link]"

### Saturday–Sunday — Post-GW1 wrap

**Post:** "GW1 in the books. Here's how live rank moved through the weekend" (screenshot or data from the app). Tag relevant FPL accounts.

**FPL Discords:** Share the post-GW wrap in relevant Discord channels with a brief "here's what our live rank tracking showed this GW" framing.

### Key operating rules for the co-founder

- **Batch on Sunday, don't post in real-time** (except genuine live-moment tweets during matches).
- **One link per post maximum.** Twitter algorithm suppresses posts where links are too prominent.
- **Engage first, promote second** — reply to 10 FPL tweets before checking own scheduled post performance each day.
- **Never cross-post** the exact same content to Reddit and Twitter on the same day.
